# NEFILIM

is a system built to notice the signals people miss in themselves before they give in to them. Where Noviembre is the friend who stays with you, NEFILIM is the presence that observes patterns and can anticipate decline before it happens.

---

## The problem

Deterioration rarely happens all at once. It builds — a few nights of bad sleep, stress eating, more time avoiding a task, small signals that pile up quietly until the day someone finally breaks. Most people don't notice the pattern until they're already in it. NEFILIM exists to catch that pattern earlier.

---

## What it does today

Current version: **v3 — Streamlit dashboard.**

- CLI input flow
- Record validation
- State detection
- History storage
- Trend review
- Streamlit dashboard

## Why I built it

I'd spent time reading psychology books and taking notes on patterns in how people behave under pressure, long before I thought of it as a project. At some point I asked myself: what if I turned that habit of observing into something real?

Not to diagnose, not to replace anyone who's trained for that. Just to be the system that watches closely enough to say something before it's too late.
